Hedgehogs are small, nocturnal mammals known for the distinctive spines that cover their backs, offering protection from predators. They spend their nights foraging for insects, berries, and other small treats, using their keen sense of smell to explore. Despite their prickly appearance, hedgehogs are gentle and curious creatures that have charmed people all around the world.


Hedgehogs can be found throughout much of the UK, especially in gardens, hedgerows, woodlands, and parks where they can forage safely at night. They prefer areas with plenty of ground cover, such as leaf piles, bushes, or log stacks, which offer shelter and protection. Encouraging wildlife-friendly spaces in neighbourhoods has become an important effort to help hedgehog populations thrive across towns and countryside alike.
