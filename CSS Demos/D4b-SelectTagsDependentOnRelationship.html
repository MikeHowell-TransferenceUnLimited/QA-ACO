    A Heading

    Marbles have been a favorite childhood pastime for generations, with their bright colors and smooth glass surfaces inviting endless games and friendly competition. Each marble seems to have its own personality, whether it’s a shimmering “cat’s eye” or a bold, solid-colored shooter. Collecting and trading them is just as fun as playing, making marbles a simple but timeless treasure.

    The iconic “cat’s eye” inside a marble is created by injecting a thin stream of colored molten glass into a clear glass sphere while it’s still hot and pliable. As the marble is rolled and shaped, the colored glass twists and stretches into the curved, feather-like pattern that resembles a cat’s pupil. Once cooled and polished, the design becomes permanently suspended within the marble, giving each one its own distinct and mesmerizing look.
