Pinewood is lightweight, versatile, and easily planed, making it ideal for furniture frames and interior paneling. Its pale grain accepts stain with warmth, though knots may bleed if not properly sealed. From architraves to shelving units, pine’s strength-to-weight ratio ensures a dependable balance between durability and economy.
