Petunias are cheerful flowering plants that bring vivid color to gardens and window boxes throughout the summer. Their trumpet-shaped blooms come in shades ranging from soft pastels to deep purples and vibrant reds. Easy to grow from seed or bedding plants, they thrive in sunny spots with well-drained soil. With regular watering and deadheading, petunias will reward you with continuous blossoms until the first frost.

Nasturtiums are bright, fast-growing annuals known for their vivid orange, yellow, and red blooms. Their round leaves and trailing stems make them ideal for hanging baskets or garden borders. Both the flowers and leaves are edible, adding a peppery flavor to salads. Thriving in poor soil and sunny conditions, nasturtiums bring effortless charm and color to any garden.
